Shipping & Returns

Orders ship within 1-2 business days via ground carrier. Standard shipping is free on orders over $75; expedited shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee. Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email.

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unworn and has its original tags attached. Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order so you get your replacement as fast as possible. To start a return, contact support with your order number and we'll email you a prepaid label.

Final-sale items (marked as such at checkout) are not eligible for return or exchange. Defective items are covered under our one-year warranty regardless of the return window.
