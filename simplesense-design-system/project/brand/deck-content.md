## The big idea
"Imagine a wise shopkeeper who has run 500 stores — sitting right next to you,
every Monday." Turn a store's data into a short, ranked to‑do list that makes the
owner money — the judgment of a seasoned CEO, at software price.

## Problem
"Merchants aren't missing data. They're drowning in it." Shopify, GA4, Meta,
Google each tell a different story. 10–15 hrs/week reconciling numbers. The hard
part isn't seeing the data — it's deciding what to change.

## Solution
A virtual ex‑CEO / ex‑CMO for your store. Three verbs:
- **Sees what tools miss** — geo concentration, Pareto economics, cohorts,
  cart‑abandon, slow inventory.
- **Thinks like an operator** — connects spend, customer quality and profit.
- **Prescribes the next move** — "Build this Klaviyo segment, launch this flow,
  turn on these pickup rules" — with impact ranges.

## How it works (3 steps)
01 **Ingest** — connect Shopify, pull 3–5+ yrs of data.
02 **Analyze** — operator‑grade AI runs cohort/geo/Pareto/retention/inventory.
03 **Prescribe** — ranked actions + expected impact + one‑click export.

## Product in action (signature examples)
- **Geographic concentration:** "82% of your customers come from within 5 miles
  of your physical locations." → geo‑fence ads to 5‑mile radius; turn on local
  pickup; shift budget local.
- **Pareto customer economics:** "Your top 20% of customers drive ~70% of
  revenue." → build the top‑20% Klaviyo segment; launch a VIP flow; double down
  on the channel that produced them.
