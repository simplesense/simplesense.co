## 2. The moat, correctly framed

Your materials make **operator credibility the moat** ("Operator Credibility as the Moat"). That is the wrong load-bearing claim for two reasons: (1) a moat that *is* the founder is fragile — it doesn't compound and doesn't survive the founder; and (2) it leans on the very pedigree claim that needs correcting. Investors discount "the founder is the moat."

**Reframe — two layers:**

- **Founder credibility = the wedge (trust + distribution), not the moat.** Your operator background is real and valuable: it lowers CAC (authentic community presence), lowers the activation barrier to 5+ years of sensitive data ("an operator built this, and we never sell or train on your data"), and lets you speak with authority generic tools can't. That is an *acquisition and trust advantage* — position it there.

- **The durable, compounding moat = the outcome-data flywheel + switching costs.** Every prescription is tagged with the *measured* revenue lift it produced. Over an install base, that yields cross-merchant benchmarks ("stores like yours that did X saw Y") a new entrant cannot replicate without the same install base and outcome loop. More merchants → more outcome data → sharper prescriptions → better results → more merchants. Layer on switching costs (once a merchant wires a VIP Klaviyo flow, BOPIS rules, and geo-fenced campaigns to your prescriptions, you're embedded in operations). *This* is what compounds and survives — and it's consistent with the flywheel and differentiation slides in your other deck.

> **Important consistency note:** do **not** claim "proprietary AI models." Simple Sense does the math deterministically in code and uses an existing frontier model only to explain and rank — grounded strictly in the store's real numbers. Claiming proprietary models invites a technical reviewer to dismantle you; the data-loop framing is both honest and stronger.

## 10. The ask & valuation — *corrected*

**Raise: $1.0M pre-seed (with $1.25–1.5M as the runway-buffer option).**
**Instrument: post-money SAFE, valuation cap, no discount.**
**Target cap: ~$10M (defensible range $8–12M).**
**Stage: pre-seed** (concept / pre-traction) — *not* seed.

Why this corrects the uploaded $400–500k @ $4–6M pre:
- **$400–500k is under-capitalized.** With ~30–40% going to founder runway + first hire, only ~$150–250k is left for build + infra + GTM. That funds a beta and maybe 80–150 customers *on fumes*, then forces a re-raise from weakness. $1.0–1.5M buys an earlier technical hire, real GTM, and runway to **150–250 paying customers + ROI case studies** — which is what unlocks a *strong* seed.
- **$4–6M pre-money is below market** — it under-values you. 2025 modal pre-seed SAFE caps are **~$10M** for sub-$1M raises ([Carta](https://carta.com/data/state-of-pre-seed-2025/)); the median pre-seed pre-money is ~$7.7M. A ~$10M cap on $1.0M is **~10% dilution** — clean, founder-friendly, and *more* favorable than your $4–6M proposal.
- **It's the right amount vs the alternatives you've considered:** not $5M (the other memo — far too much for pre-traction; it sets an unclearable Series A bar), not $400–500k (too little). $1.0–1.5M is the disciplined middle the market supports for your profile.

**A strategic fork worth naming (your own numbers surface it):** your sober financials show a credible path to **break-even on very little capital**. That gives you leverage and two honest options — (a) raise a lean **$1.0–1.5M pre-seed** to go faster and de-risk the hire + GTM, or (b) **bootstrap toward profitability** and raise a larger seed later from maximum strength. Either is defensible. What you should *not* do is raise $400–500k — it's the worst of both: enough to dilute, too little to meaningfully accelerate.

**The $5M is your *next* round** — a real seed in 12–18 months, priced on traction (live on the App Store, ~250–400 paying customers, ~$0.6–0.9M ARR exit run-rate, signed agency channel, ROI case studies). That's the Triple Whale / Polar path.

**On the slide:** *"Raising $1.0M pre-seed. Instrument and valuation to be set with counsel."* Carry the cap verbally: *"$1.0M on a post-money SAFE, targeting a ~$10M cap, with a clear path to a $5M seed on traction."*
